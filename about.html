About Us

Emmanuel Senior Secondary School, Tonk is committed to delivering holistic education that combines academic excellence with values, creativity, discipline and leadership.

Founded in 1976, the school aims to shape confident, responsible and future-ready students through quality teaching, strong values and a nurturing environment.

Our Vision

To become a premier educational institution known for excellence, discipline and integrity.

Our Mission

To nurture knowledge, confidence, creativity and leadership among students.

Our Values

Excellence, integrity, respect, honesty and compassion in all learning experiences.
