About Us

Who We Are

Emmanuel Senior Secondary School, established in 1976, is a co-educational, English-medium school located on Jaipur Road, Near Bus Stand, Kali Paltan, Tonk, Rajasthan (PIN 304001). The school is affiliated to the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE), Affiliation ID: 1730313, and is managed by the E.E. & Charitable Society.

Our Vision

To become a leading educational institution that develops knowledgeable, confident, responsible, and successful individuals.

Our Mission

  • Provide quality education to every student.
  • Encourage creativity and independent thinking.
  • Develop discipline, honesty, and respect.
  • Promote sports, arts, technology, and innovation.
  • Prepare students for higher education and future careers.
